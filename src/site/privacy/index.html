Privacy

Murder Sudoku 3D is a free puzzle game without accounts, ads, or tracking. This page explains the little data that is involved.

Last updated: October 11, 2026

On the website

The website uses PostHog, hosted in the EU, for anonymous usage statistics, such as which pages are viewed and how many games are started or solved. These numbers show what to improve.

  • No cookies. The statistics work without cookies.
  • No personal profiles. Visits are not linked to you, and your IP address is not stored.
  • No ads, and no selling of data. The data is used only to improve the game.
  • Do Not Track is respected. If your browser sends a Do Not Track signal, no statistics are collected at all.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data, such as your IP address, to keep the service secure. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Your progress

There are no accounts. Your progress, settings, and language choice are saved only on your own device, in your browser's local storage. Nothing is sent to a server. Clearing your browser data deletes them.

Feedback

The feedback button opens a prefilled form on GitHub in your browser. If you submit it, your message is public and the GitHub Privacy Statement applies.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on GitHub.
